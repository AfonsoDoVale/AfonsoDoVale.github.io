Substellar population of the young massive cluster RCW 36 in Vela ↗

A. R. G. do Brito do Vale, K. Mužić, H. Bouy, V. Almendros-Abad, A. Bayo, D. Capela, A. Scholz, A. Bik, G. Suárez, L. Cieza, K. Peña Ramírez, E. Bertin & R. Schödel

Astronomy & Astrophysics · 706, A149 · First-author publication

Journal article ↗arXiv ↗
Research summary

A study of the initial mass function and substellar population of RCW 36, combining deep HAWK-I/VLT observations with archival photometry and Gaia kinematics. The analysis examines cluster membership, the low-mass population, and the ratio of stars to brown dwarfs.
