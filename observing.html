Cerro Tololo Inter-American Observatory

A census of Chamaeleon I and II down to the planetary mass regime

Proposal 2026A-591642 · Principal investigator: Hervé Bouy, who carried out the NEWFIRM observations. The programme combines wide-area near-infrared imaging of Cha I and Cha II with archival observations to measure proper motions and search for members down to planetary masses. Co-investigators: Afonso do Brito do Vale, Núria Miret-Roig, Koraljka Mužić, Javier Olivares and Sean Points.
