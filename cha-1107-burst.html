Recovering the accretion burst of Cha 1107

The known accretion burst of Cha 1107–7626, recovered in our multi-epoch DANCe photometry.

Our multi-epoch DANCe catalogue also recovers photometric evidence of the known accretion burst in Cha 1107–7626, reported by Almendros-Abad et al. (2025). The optical brightening in its archival light curve shows how the same observations used for membership selection can trace the evolution of an accreting planetary-mass object. This recovery complements the published discovery and motivates a search for comparable young, disk-bearing objects.

Part of the Cha-I DANCe project
